# Retired lexical planner (research checkout only)

These three modules are the historical lexical planner, planning context, and planning stages formerly installed under `src/schemarouter/`. They remain available only for reproducing existing comparative research and regression fixtures; the wheel packages only `src/schemarouter`. Do not add imports from this directory to the product or use it as a production fallback.

The restored paper planner lives in the product's paper planning modules. Historical lexical results do not constitute validation of that planner. The former v0.17 versus lexical candidate wheel workflow remains in Git history along with its comparator; it cannot be interpreted as a current-product wheel regression after the public lexical API is removed.

The historical flat `PlanRequest`, analyzer output `QueryIntent` and request literals `RetrievalMode` and `FallbackScope` are defined in `requests.py` and the two retrieval facades in `../research_legacy_runtime.py`. The installed request contract is `PaperPlanRequest`; the product never imports the historical envelope or its dispatch.

Fixed Top-K typed-state filtering is archived in `state_retrieval.py`, alongside the separate historical backfill comparator in `../research_legacy_state_backfill.py`. The product retains declared state and precondition evaluation in `schemarouter.execution_state`; those checks do not rank or execute capabilities.

Historical lightweight route DTOs live in `route_models.py`; their field-scope projection lives on `LegacySchemaRouter` in `../research_legacy_runtime.py`. Both retain the existing alias/metadata redaction and authority checks while the installed package no longer exposes lightweight lexical route ranking.

`retrieval_abstention.py`, `retrieval_execution_gate.py` and `selective_invocation.py` preserve the historical calibrated-score and shortlist execution experiment, including all no-call, scope, provenance, fallback and detached-plan checks. They are not installed and cannot become an Algorithm 1 fallback. Paper planning uses its own current-source typed receipt and plan witness, and the same registered executor enforces actual authorization, operation policy, schema validation and binding readiness. The shared declared operation-fit validator remains installed.

Full-schema lexical shortlist DTOs (`CapabilityCandidate`, `CapabilityRetrieval`) are archived in `candidate_models.py`. Their full-candidate projection and recognized-score-label redaction now live in `../research_legacy_runtime.py`. Common source field/parameter masking, source authority witnesses and actual executor validation remain in the product. The legacy DTOs use the checkout-only explanation types in `plan_models.py`.

`plan_models.py` retains the historical `ScoreComponent`, selection-source/reason literals and `PlanExplanation` classes. Research-only ToolCall, FallbackRoute and ExecutionPlan subclasses preserve the previous nested score/explanation JSON while sharing the product's execution fields, limits and fallback validation. The installed ToolCall does not accept these extra fields; the paper Rank receipt and original-source execution witness are unchanged. Research validators use the known historical schema rather than dropping unchecked metadata. Old serialized lexical plans are research inputs, not current-product execution authority.

The standalone G5 E0 one-hop execution experiment is archived in `../research_legacy_g5_execution.py`. Its original registered-executor calls and source/policy/observation checks remain reproducible in a checkout, but it is not an installed execution entry point. Preserve the canonical graph index, Algorithm 1 typed plan and registered executor; the historical observed-binding validation is retained in the research checkout. The historical E0/E1 demo evidence is not current-product or independent E2 qualification.

The generalized exact-depth 2–4-edge G5 comparator lives in `../research_legacy_g5_deep_paths.py`, invoked as `lookup_bounded_native_paths(index, router, query, ...)`. The installed index no longer exposes `lookup_bounded_paths`. Its canonical one-/two-hop methods remain installed. The shared-root fork selector and its result types are now checkout-only in `../research_legacy_native_fork.py`, invoked as `lookup_native_shared_root_forks(index, router, query, ...)`. The original bounded private-arm collector and its distinct receipt now live in `../research_legacy_fork_arm.py`; historical fork/observation and instrumentation consumers use that checkout-only implementation. The installed one-hop lookup has no private fork flags or internal-arm receipt status. The shared product `PaperFieldRoute` and typed call validation stay in `paper_typed_tool_plan.py`. Historical depth experiments retain their source/authorization/visit-budget assertions and do not qualify the current product.

Standalone graph SCC/cycle/satisfiability diagnostics are preserved in `../research_legacy_capability_graph_analysis.py` with their prior algorithms and regression fixtures. The product keeps the exact original typed edge builder, incremental updater, immutable graph model and adjacency indexes; these underpin source-native paper dependency planning and graph publication/drift checks.

The historical external ranking callbacks are preserved in `../research_legacy_posting_rank.py` and `../research_legacy_g5_rank.py` as explicit source-checkout lookup functions. Their pre-retirement lookup implementations preserve bounded candidate menus, detached records, strict offset validation, and post-callback source/scope checks. These are archived comparators, not a production extension mechanism. Product lookup methods accept only the reviewed paper Rank policy and retain strict-K unranked abstention; private typed fork-arm behavior belongs exclusively to research. The product never imports either comparator.

Historical callback ranking receipts now have their own dataclasses in the two archived comparator modules, with the exact pre-retirement fields and statuses. Installed index receipts no longer expose `experimental_ranked` or `ranker_abstained_unverified`. The existing deterministic paper Rank receipt flag and `ranked_unverified` status remain, while the historical non-executing shared-root proof is now checkout-only.

The earlier exact-alias session entrypoints `discover()` and `compile()` are now checkout-only functions in `../research_legacy_field_session.py`. The installed session provides `compile_understood` and schema-independent `acompile_query`; inspection, catalog revision, refresh and guarded direct execution remain. The offered Concept-ID model protocol is checkout-only in `../research_legacy_offered_query.py`. The former query-alias Field Union retrieval and typed compilation now live in `../research_legacy_paper_field_retrieval.py` and `../research_legacy_paper_typed_plan.py`. The archived observation and consumer execution paths explicitly use those comparators. Product `PaperFieldRoute`, `PaperTypedToolPlan` and shared call-scope checks remain; no installed query-only alternative selector or shared-root fork wrapper.

The raw-query alias resolver and native index convenience wrappers are preserved in `../research_legacy_concept_retrieval.py`: `resolve_native_concepts`, `lookup_posting_concepts` and `lookup_graph_concepts`. The indexed concept graph menu and its receipt types live in `../research_legacy_indexed_concept_graph.py`; the earlier lexical comparator remains distinct in `../research_legacy_concept_graph.py`. Installed owner concept catalog/contract merging and named-source alias utilities remain shared by Algorithm 1. These checkout-only menus do not qualify current product model performance.

The A9 understood one-/two-hop producer-to-consumer execution entrypoint and receipt are now checkout-only in `../research_legacy_paper_graph_execution.py`. The historical body, registered executor, live source/policy checks and observation binding validation are retained. Algorithm 1 selects independent source tools and does not discover or execute dependency DAGs. The current product execution methods and typed source proofs are unchanged; the remaining typed graph planning policy is a separate audit item.

The named and unnamed G5 one-/two-hop planner is now checkout-only in `../research_legacy_paper_graph_plan.py`. Its original implementation uses the same owner, source, graph, field and Rank proofs; `compile_research_understood` provides the historical prepared-session adapter to research consumers. Installed `compile_understood`, `acompile_query` and `compile_model_query` no longer accept dependency discovery or hop-count options and compile independent source plans. Historical graph pipeline profiling is not a measurement of the current product. Alternate direct Rank scoring and lower-level graph Rank remain separate audit items.

The original G5 owner-ranked one-/two-hop source-proof collectors and mixed-depth comparator are now checkout-only in `../research_legacy_graph_rank.py`. Their original bounded counts, source/ACL/health and Rank mutation checks, and deterministic ranking receipts remain available to historical experiments. The installed native graph index keeps only its original unranked K5 evidence and abstention path, with no Rank arguments or ranked receipt statuses. Direct paper Rank remains installed and unchanged; typed direct scoring is still a separate audit item.

The historical typed exact/partial direct comparator, unfocused Top-K policy, complete original posting lookup and their receipt now live in `../research_legacy_direct_rank.py`; its historical direct compiler lives in `../research_legacy_direct_plan.py`. Both retain the previous source/ACL/health and policy rechecks. Historical G5 and offered-ID callers use the separate research policy. The installed `PaperNativeRankPolicy` accepts only `paper_v1` with `focus_direct=True`, and installed posting/plan calls no longer accept the alternate alias ranking inputs. Named and reviewed multi-target overrides still retain all qualified targets rather than forcing a single winner. Unranked review/diagnostic paths are a separate contract and are not another scoring comparator.

The nonexecuting G5 observed-scalar validation and its `NativeObservedArgumentEvidence` receipt are now checkout-only in `../research_legacy_graph_observation.py`. All original identity, scope, required-input, finite-scalar, projection and schema checks remain unchanged. Historical A9 and Field Union callers use the same archived helper. The installed direct Algorithm 1 planner and registered executor do not depend on this caller-observation graph contract; do not infer provider authenticity, execution authority or independent E2 from it.

The former principal-scoped graph Rank count fields are retained only in the original candidate shapes in `../research_legacy_graph_candidates.py`. The historical owner-Rank, callback and private fork-arm collectors return those distinct DTOs; archived ranking tests and benchmarks keep the previous field order, defaults and equality semantics. Installed one-/two-hop candidates contain only the original nonexecuting source/edge proof and no optional Rank counts. The public unranked observation/execute research entrypoints continue to require exact installed candidate membership; the shared internal scalar checker accepts either shape only after the caller proves its own exact current edge.

The historical `MAX_RESEARCH_RANKABLE_PATHS = 32` budget is now owned by `../research_legacy_graph_candidates.py`. Its callback, fork, deep-path and owner-Rank comparators retain the same original value. The installed index independently names the existing `_MAX_TARGET_ROUTE_PREFILTER` and `_MAX_TWO_HOP_QUALIFIED_PATHS` guards (both 32); one-hop still reports the full qualified count while withholding candidates above K5. These are source-proof safety budgets, not a model exposure or ranking policy. No limit, exception boundary or selection algorithm changed.

Installed one-/two-hop unranked graph results no longer carry `independent_quality_evidence` or `calibrated_no_fit`: a structural source proof cannot itself establish empirical quality or calibrated global absence. Historical Rank, fork, deep-path and concept-graph research receipts retain their original metadata and wire shape. The installed `executable_authority` field remains an explicit serialized false marker (`Literal[False]`) and rejects any other constructor value, including dataclass replacement. This marker does not authorize execution; actual registered-executor, policy, schema, source and binding checks are unchanged. Current-source benchmarks assert the absence of the retired claims instead of reading them as false.
