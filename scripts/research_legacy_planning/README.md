# Retired lexical planner (research checkout only)

These three modules are the historical lexical planner, planning context, and planning stages formerly installed under `src/schemarouter/`. They remain available only for reproducing existing comparative research and regression fixtures; the wheel packages only `src/schemarouter`. Do not add imports from this directory to the product or use it as a production fallback.

The restored paper planner lives in the product's paper planning modules. Historical lexical results do not constitute validation of that planner. The former v0.17 versus lexical candidate wheel workflow remains in Git history along with its comparator; it cannot be interpreted as a current-product wheel regression after the public lexical API is removed.

The historical flat `PlanRequest`, analyzer output `QueryIntent` and request literals `RetrievalMode` and `FallbackScope` are defined in `requests.py` and the two retrieval facades in `../research_legacy_runtime.py`. The installed request contract is `PaperPlanRequest`; the product never imports the historical envelope or its dispatch.

Fixed Top-K typed-state filtering is archived in `state_retrieval.py`, alongside the separate historical backfill comparator in `../research_legacy_state_backfill.py`. The product retains declared state and precondition evaluation in `schemarouter.execution_state`; those checks do not rank or execute capabilities.

Historical lightweight route DTOs live in `route_models.py`; their field-scope projection lives on `LegacySchemaRouter` in `../research_legacy_runtime.py`. Both retain the existing alias/metadata redaction and authority checks while the installed package no longer exposes lightweight lexical route ranking.

`retrieval_abstention.py`, `retrieval_execution_gate.py` and `selective_invocation.py` preserve the historical calibrated-score and shortlist execution experiment, including all no-call, scope, provenance, fallback and detached-plan checks. They are not installed and cannot become an Algorithm 1 fallback. Paper planning uses its own current-source typed receipt and plan witness, and the same registered executor enforces actual authorization, operation policy, schema validation and binding readiness. The shared declared operation-fit validator remains installed.

Full-schema lexical shortlist DTOs (`CapabilityCandidate`, `CapabilityRetrieval`) are archived in `candidate_models.py`. Their full-candidate projection and recognized-score-label redaction now live in `../research_legacy_runtime.py`. Common source field/parameter masking, source authority witnesses and actual executor validation remain in the product. The legacy DTOs use the checkout-only explanation types in `plan_models.py`.

`plan_models.py` retains the historical `ScoreComponent`, selection-source/reason literals and `PlanExplanation` classes. Research-only ToolCall, FallbackRoute and ExecutionPlan subclasses preserve the previous nested score/explanation JSON while sharing the product's execution fields, limits and fallback validation. The installed ToolCall does not accept these extra fields; the paper Rank receipt and original-source execution witness are unchanged. Research validators use the known historical schema rather than dropping unchecked metadata. Old serialized lexical plans are research inputs, not current-product execution authority.

The standalone G5 E0 one-hop execution experiment is archived in `../research_legacy_g5_execution.py`. Its original registered-executor calls and source/policy/observation checks remain reproducible in a checkout, but it is not an installed execution entry point. Preserve the canonical graph index, Algorithm 1 typed plan, internal observed-binding validation and registered executor. The historical E0/E1 demo evidence is not current-product or independent E2 qualification.

The generalized exact-depth 2–4-edge G5 comparator lives in `../research_legacy_g5_deep_paths.py`, invoked as `lookup_bounded_native_paths(index, router, query, ...)`. The installed index no longer exposes `lookup_bounded_paths`. Its canonical one-/two-hop methods remain installed. The shared-root fork selector and its result types are now checkout-only in `../research_legacy_native_fork.py`, invoked as `lookup_native_shared_root_forks(index, router, query, ...)`. The bounded private arm collector remains temporarily installed for those historical proof and measurement consumers; retiring it is a separate audit. The shared product `PaperFieldRoute` and typed call validation stay in `paper_typed_tool_plan.py`. Historical depth experiments retain their source/authorization/visit-budget assertions and do not qualify the current product.

Standalone graph SCC/cycle/satisfiability diagnostics are preserved in `../research_legacy_capability_graph_analysis.py` with their prior algorithms and regression fixtures. The product keeps the exact original typed edge builder, incremental updater, immutable graph model and adjacency indexes; these underpin source-native paper dependency planning and graph publication/drift checks.

The historical external ranking callbacks are preserved in `../research_legacy_posting_rank.py` and `../research_legacy_g5_rank.py` as explicit source-checkout lookup functions. Their pre-retirement lookup implementations preserve bounded candidate menus, detached records, strict offset validation, and post-callback source/scope checks. These are archived comparators, not a production extension mechanism. Product lookup methods accept only the reviewed paper Rank policy and keep the existing unranked and private typed fork-arm behavior. The product never imports either comparator.

Historical callback ranking receipts now have their own dataclasses in the two archived comparator modules, with the exact pre-retirement fields and statuses. Installed index receipts no longer expose `experimental_ranked` or `ranker_abstained_unverified`. The existing deterministic paper Rank receipt flag and `ranked_unverified` status remain, while the historical non-executing shared-root proof is now checkout-only.

The earlier exact-alias session entrypoints `discover()` and `compile()` are now checkout-only functions in `../research_legacy_field_session.py`. The installed session provides `compile_understood` and schema-independent `acompile_query`; inspection, catalog revision, refresh and guarded direct execution remain. The offered Concept-ID model protocol is checkout-only in `../research_legacy_offered_query.py`. The former query-alias Field Union retrieval and typed compilation now live in `../research_legacy_paper_field_retrieval.py` and `../research_legacy_paper_typed_plan.py`. The archived observation and consumer execution paths explicitly use those comparators. Product `PaperFieldRoute`, `PaperTypedToolPlan` and shared call-scope checks remain; no installed query-only alternative selector or shared-root fork wrapper.
