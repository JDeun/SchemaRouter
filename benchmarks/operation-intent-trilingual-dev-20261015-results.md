## Fixed development evaluation

Nine English action descriptions were ranked against **72 newly authored requests**:
**54 supported** (18 per language; 2 per operation per language), **18 unsupported**
(6 per language: negatives, metalinguistic/explanatory, multiple actions,
and out-of-domain tasks). The supported-vs-unsupported denominators must never
be combined in an accuracy metric. No realistic 100/250/500-tool catalog or
downstream tool execution was measured in this experiment.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Conservative lexical hypothesis | **52/54 (96.3%)** | 2/54 (3.7%) | **0/54** | **0/18** | **0.0256** |

### Observable errors and limits

- The lexical baseline abstained on `supported-en-search-1` ("Locate..." is not
  covered by the fixed explicit-action lexicon) and `supported-ko-list-1`
  (the adjective "등록된" collided with the `create` verb marker).
  Changing the lexicon **after seeing these cases** would be development tuning,
  not independent validation.
- E5 misclassified Korean research-paper search as `history` or `lookup`,
  and Japanese `lookup` as `search` or `history`.
- MiniLM made numerous Korean errors, including `update` -> `directions`,
  `delete` -> `history`, and `search` -> `directions`.
- All unsupported cases returned a forced embedding top-1 operation.
  **This is intrinsic to this unthresholded ranking interface, not proof
  that a separately designed abstaining classifier would always fail.**
- At *illustrative* cosine 0.6, MiniLM would reject **51/54 (94.4%)**
  supported examples while rejecting 18/18 unsupported; E5 would still
  accept 18/18 unsupported and misclassify 13/54 supported examples.
  E5 and MiniLM cosine scales are not comparable or calibrated.
  **No similarity threshold is selected or authorized for production.**

### Decision

On this authored, intentionally easy set, the frozen conservative lexical
baseline is a useful **advisory hypothesis only**, whereas the evaluated
single-vector embedding ranking is insufficient as a standalone intent
classifier. These findings do not contradict MiniLM's favorable *candidate
retrieval* results in a different task. They imply task-specific measurement,
independent negation/multi-action detection, and proper calibrated rejection
are required before any broader operation classification is trusted.

**Keep #1339, #1291, #1346 OPEN; keep v0.18 PR #1356 HOLD.**
