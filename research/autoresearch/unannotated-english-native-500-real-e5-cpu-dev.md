## Predeclared decision rule and honest failure modes

Do not promote E5 from Top-K recall alone. It must (a) improve on KO/JA correct native field/route coverage without significant EN regressions, (b) not create unsafe unsupported false accepts, (c) fit latency/RSS budget against the lexical baseline, and (d) pass downstream native typed tool argument + grounding and no-fit task-success tests. This script measures (a) and some of (c), documents (b) as **known uncalibrated** and cannot measure (d).
